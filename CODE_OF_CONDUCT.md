# Code of conduct

## Our standard

CompactVIO-UAV discussions should be rigorous, respectful, and safe for people with different
levels of robotics and machine-learning experience.

Expected behavior includes:

- explain technical disagreements with evidence and reproducible examples;
- distinguish measured results, inference, and opinion;
- acknowledge uncertainty and correct mistakes without personal attacks;
- protect private recordings, credentials, unpublished artifacts, and personal information;
- give credit for ideas, code, data, and research contributions;
- keep safety limitations visible when discussing navigation or physical systems.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct,
doxxing, deliberate misrepresentation of results, pressure to hide failed experiments, or
publishing another person's sensitive data.

## Scope

This policy applies to issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, project-controlled community
spaces, and public interactions made while representing the project.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository owner through the account hosting this
repository. Do not place personal or sensitive details in a public issue. The owner may remove
content, close or lock discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when needed
to protect the community or the integrity of the research record.

Good-faith reports and corrections will not be penalized. Deliberately false reports or
retaliation against a reporter are themselves violations.

## Contribution-status note

The repository does not yet have a selected source license. Until contribution terms are
resolved and this policy is updated, external contributions are discussion-only and external pull
requests are not accepted. This status does not limit respectful issue reports or documentation
feedback.
